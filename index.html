PROCESS ARCHITECTURE

Systematic outlier capture built for asymmetry

MICRO CTA is designed around a simple institutional truth: meaningful portfolio convexity rarely comes from frequent forecasting accuracy. It comes from structured participation in persistent moves while keeping losses contained when the market offers no edge.

The engine prioritizes trend following, position discipline, and consistent rule execution over narrative interpretation. This means accepting many small failed attempts in exchange for rare but significant directional expansions that reshape the equity curve.

Rather than predicting turning points, the process observes price behavior, responds to regime shifts, and maintains asymmetric exposure when conditions align. The objective is not to be right often. The objective is to be positioned when outliers emerge.
