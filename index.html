Strategy Architecture

A calm process for rare moves that matter.

MICRO CTA is built around an institutional observation: the most meaningful changes in portfolio outcomes often come from a small number of exceptional trends rather than frequent directional forecasting. The system is designed to remain present for those outlier episodes without depending on prediction.

This requires accepting many modest losses and short-lived attempts. Capital preservation, exposure sizing, and disciplined exits are not defensive accessories to the process. They are the mechanism that keeps the strategy alive long enough to capture its rare but transformative winners.

Trend following is treated as a repeatable decision framework rather than a narrative. Signals are executed systematically, risk is framed before conviction, and the portfolio responds to evidence instead of opinion. The objective is simple: contain damage when the market offers nothing, expand with conviction when persistence emerges.

In practice, this creates an asymmetric return profile shaped by small losses, larger winners, and consistency of execution. The edge comes from behavior, not prediction. From process, not noise.
